I'm a Hamburg-based ~~independent open source software developer~~ ~~tortured artist and creative technologist~~
mostly eukaryotic multicellular form of life operating at the intersection of language, systems thinking, and
written as well as visual art.

In technology, I specialise in crafting high-quality software for UNIX and Windows platforms, with a focus on
aesthetic engineering - creating expressive code and technical documentation that is as readable and composable as
a well-woven poem. I simultaneously strongly espouse an *error/failure-oriented* attitude in engineering, designing
and implementing systems with a primary view towards how and when they fail as well as how to adequately address
failure, as well as openness towards exploration of novel pathways and perspectives, particularly those that provide
an increase in breadth of modes of expression and the structural collapse of questions in need of unasking. I favour
both methodical, structural approaches with a sound phenomenology as well as calling the same into question when it
is gainsaid by empirical evidence, no matter how challenging its status may be.

I have contributed to a [number of open source projects](https://www.luciaillanes.de/projects) and continue to maintain
a [GitHub](https://github.com/lalbornoz/). My Curriculum Vitae is available on [my website](https://www.luciaillanes.de)
both as a [PDF](https://www.luciaillanes.de/assets/Curriculum%20Vitae%20(English).pdf) as well as in
[LaTeX](https://www.luciaillanes.de/assets/CurriculumVitae.tex).

I have a passionate, lifelong interest in languages, nurtured by the multilingual and -cultural environment that I
grew up in, beginning with my native *Spanish (Chilean/Andalusian dialect)* and *German (Northern Germany/Hamburg dialect)*
as well as *English (British English)* at CEFR level C1 according to personal judgment and, as a classical language,
*Classical Arabic* and a smattering of dialects of *Spoken Arabic*, garnished with undertones of *Akkadian* and *Persian*.

In creativity, I am drawn to weaving higher-order patterns from matters of little in the way of connection and often
much in the way of contradiction, with a particular inclination towards emptiness, dissociation, and apophasis, drawing
from fields such as Old Mesopotamia, Classical Arabic literature, mysticism, magical realism, gender identity, non-dualism,
kade, neutrinos, clinical psychology and psychiatry, and the indelibly indelicate flavour of my puzzlingly continuing
existence. In literature, I greatly prefer the expression and exploration of *subjectivity of experience* over
the mere replication of ostensible objectivity, often through the modes of liminality, transition, and non-being.

Additionally, I am equally strongly drawn to systems and dynamic processes of dazzling dimensionality, laden with
recurrent loops, bearing state networks that silently sing the stillness of a garden perturbed by the gradients and
curves of Classical Persian music, especially those found in biology in general and endocrinology in particular as
well as whatever a long walk or indulging in a film of interest may yield when serving as the means of phenomenological
archaeology.
